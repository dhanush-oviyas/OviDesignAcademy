01Course & Learning

01 What is UX/UI Design?+

UX (User Experience) Design focuses on creating products that are easy, useful, and enjoyable to use. UI (User Interface) Design focuses on the visual design of websites, mobile apps, and software. Together, UX/UI Designers create digital experiences used by millions of people every day.

02 Who can join the UX/UI Design course?+

Our course is suitable for:

  • College Students
  • Fresh Graduates
  • Working Professionals
  • Career Switchers
  • Non-IT Professionals
  • Entrepreneurs
  • Housewives restarting their careers

No prior design experience is required.

03 Is this course suitable for beginners?+

Yes. We start from the fundamentals and gradually cover advanced UX/UI concepts, ensuring beginners can confidently build their design skills.

04 Do I need coding knowledge?+

No. UX/UI Design does not require coding. We focus on design thinking, user research, wireframing, prototyping, and AI-powered design workflows.

05 What software will I learn?+

Students learn industry-standard tools including:

  • Figma
  • FigJam
  • AI-powered Design Tools
  • ChatGPT
  • Claude AI
  • Prototyping Tools
  • UX Research Tools
06 What is the duration of the course?+

Depending on the program:

  • UX UI + Ai Vibe Design – 3 Months
  • Advanced UX/UI + AI Leadership Program – 5 Weeks
07 Is the training practical?+

Yes. More than 80% of the course is hands-on, with real-world projects, assignments, workshops, and portfolio building.

08 Will I work on real projects?+

Yes. Students create multiple portfolio projects based on real-world business problems, preparing them for interviews and freelance opportunities.

09 Will I build a professional portfolio?+

Absolutely. Portfolio creation is one of the most important parts of our program. You'll create UX Case Studies and industry-standard portfolio projects.

10 Do you teach AI in UX/UI Design?+

Yes. Our updated curriculum includes AI tools that help designers research, generate ideas, create wireframes, write UX content, and improve productivity.

02Career & Placement

11 What job roles can I apply for after completing the course?+

You can apply for roles including:

  • UX Designer
  • UI Designer
  • Product Designer
  • Interaction Designer
  • Visual Designer
  • UX Researcher
12 Will I get placement assistance?+

Yes. We provide:

  • Resume Building
  • Portfolio Reviews
  • Interview Preparation
  • Mock Interviews
  • Career Guidance
  • Placement Assistance
13 Will I receive interview support?+

Yes. We conduct mock interviews and guide students through common UX interview questions, portfolio presentations, and recruiter expectations.

14 Can I become a UX Designer without an engineering degree?+

Absolutely. Many successful UX Designers come from Arts, Commerce, Science, and Non-IT backgrounds.

15 What salary can a fresher expect?+

Salary depends on skills, portfolio quality, location, and company. Our goal is to help students build strong portfolios that improve their chances of securing better opportunities.

16 Can I freelance after completing the course?+

Yes. Many students begin freelancing by designing websites, mobile apps, dashboards, and digital products for startups and businesses.

17 Is UX/UI Design still a good career in 2026?+

Yes. As businesses continue investing in digital products, demand for skilled UX/UI Designers remains strong across startups, enterprises, SaaS companies, healthcare, fintech, and e-commerce.

18 Will AI replace UX/UI Designers?+

No. AI is becoming a powerful tool for designers, but human creativity, empathy, problem-solving, and strategic thinking remain essential. We teach students how to work effectively with AI to become more productive.
