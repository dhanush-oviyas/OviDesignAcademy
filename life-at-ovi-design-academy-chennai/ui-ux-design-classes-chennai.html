Community and culture

Why life at Ovi matters for better learning.

Life at Ovi is not just a gallery page. It shows how students in our Chennai studio learn through live mentor reviews, small-batch critique, workshop participation and day-to-day collaboration that pushes each portfolio further.

For many students from Chennai and other parts of Tamil Nadu, community matters as much as curriculum. That is why we combine structured learning from the core UI UX course with feedback culture, peer reviews and project showcases that keep progress visible.

If you want to see what that work leads to, visit the student works page. If you want to experience the studio before joining, contact the academy or book a free demo session from the homepage.
