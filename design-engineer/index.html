02 / The emphasis

Where the work shifts.

01 / Understand

UX Designer

Investigates user needs, maps journeys, tests ideas and improves how a product works for people. UI and implementation responsibilities depend on the team.

02 / Shape

Product Designer

Connects user needs with product goals. Typically designs flows, interfaces and systems, then works with partners to refine the shipped experience.

03 / Make

Design Engineer

Brings design craft into the build. Often prototypes in code or implements the frontend, using real interactions and constraints to improve the design.

For examples of how companies define this role, see Vercel's design engineering team and Linear's web and brand role. Their responsibilities differ, which is why this page describes a range rather than a universal job definition.
