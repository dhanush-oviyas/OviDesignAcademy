01 What is UX/UI Design?+

UX (User Experience) Design focuses on creating products that are easy, useful, and enjoyable to use. UI (User Interface) Design focuses on the visual design of websites, mobile apps, and software. Together, UX/UI Designers create digital experiences used by millions of people every day.

02 Who can join the UX/UI Design course?+

Our course is suitable for:

  • College Students
  • Fresh Graduates
  • Working Professionals
  • Career Switchers
  • Non-IT Professionals
  • Entrepreneurs
  • Housewives restarting their careers

No prior design experience is required.

03 Is this course suitable for beginners?+

Yes. We start from the fundamentals and gradually cover advanced UX/UI concepts, ensuring beginners can confidently build their design skills.

04 Do I need coding knowledge?+

No. UX/UI Design does not require coding. We focus on design thinking, user research, wireframing, prototyping, and AI-powered design workflows.

05 What software will I learn?+

Students learn industry-standard tools including:

  • Figma
  • FigJam
  • AI-powered Design Tools
  • ChatGPT
  • Claude AI
  • Prototyping Tools
  • UX Research Tools
