About the course

A practical, mentor-led path into product design.

What is this course?

The UX UI + Ai Vibe Design is a 12-week cohort program that takes you from zero to a job-ready designer. You learn the full workflow — research, wireframing, Figma craft, design systems and AI-assisted UX — by shipping 4 real case studies under weekly 1:1 mentorship, not by watching passive tutorials.

Who can join?

Built for final-year students, fresh graduates, non-IT professionals and career switchers. You need no design degree, no coding background — just curiosity and 8–10 hours a week. Over 60% of our students come from non-design fields and get placed within 3–4 months of finishing.
