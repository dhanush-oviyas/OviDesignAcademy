About the course

Built for designers ready to go deeper.

What is this course?

The Advanced UX/UI + AI Leadership Program is a 5-week, portfolio-driven track for designers who already know the fundamentals. You go deep on design systems at scale, advanced AI-assisted product workflows, and complex case studies — building a portfolio aimed squarely at senior and lead design roles.

Who can join?

Best suited for graduates of our UX UI + Ai Vibe Design course, or working designers with an existing portfolio. We review your portfolio before enrollment to make sure the pace and depth are the right fit — no absolute beginners.
